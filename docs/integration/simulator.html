Betaflight Setup Simulator

Interactive guide for setting up FrSky Taranis QX7 switches with Emax Tinyhawk.

Setup Progress

Step 1 of 5

1. QX7 Inputs Setup

First, we define inputs on the radio. Interact with the controller screen to simulate creating these inputs.

  • Assign ARM to the far left 2-position switch (SF).
  • Assign MOD to the top-left 3-pos switch (SA).
  • Assign BEP/FLP to top-right 3-pos switch (SD).
Action: Click the "MENU" button on the radio, then toggle the switches to map them.

2. QX7 Mixer Setup

Next, map inputs to channels in the Mixer screen.

  • CH5: ARM (Becomes AUX 1)
  • CH6: MOD (Becomes AUX 2)
  • CH7: BEP (Becomes AUX 3)
  • CH8: FLP (Becomes AUX 4)
Action: Click "PAGE" on radio to view mixer setup.

3. Betaflight Receiver Test

Connect to PC. Verify Betaflight reads the switches correctly.

Action: Toggle the physical switches on the controller and watch the AUX bars move in the Betaflight panel.

4. Betaflight Modes Setup

Configure logical modes based on switch positions.

  • ARM (AUX 1): Active when switch is UP.
  • ANGLE/HORIZON (AUX 2): Middle = Angle, Down = Horizon, Up = Acro (default).
  • BEEPER/FLIP (AUX 3): Middle = Flip, Down = Beeper.
Action: Observe the yellow indicators in the Modes tab as you flip switches.

5. Final Test

Disconnect USB, plug in battery (simulated), and test functions.

Action: Disarm all switches. Then Arm the drone to see props spin, or flip the Beeper switch to hear/see it beep.
